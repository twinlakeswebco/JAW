Meet the Curator

The face behind Blush & Brass

Emma-Kate Hodges

Hi, I’m Emma-Kate

I’m a vintage enthusiast from Central Kentucky and the founder of Blush & Brass Vintage. What started as a love of thrifting became a shop, a live show, and a little community of people who believe old things are the best things.

A few fun facts about me:

  • I’m a Psychology major working toward becoming a pediatric psychologist in a clinical setting.
  • I currently work as a Registered Behavior Technician (RBT) at a clinic.
  • My favorite things to thrift are Asian and Chinese pieces: ginger jars, cloisonné, teacups and saucers, Imari china, and famille rose whenever I can find it.
  • My current vintage hyper-fixation? Butter pats.
  • When I’m not sourcing, you’ll find me with my boyfriend, Tanner, and my family.

Thank you, from the bottom of my heart, for your support, encouragement, and kindness. It means so much that these beautiful vintage treasures are finding their way into homes where they’ll be loved. I hope anything you bring home from Blush & Brass fits perfectly and brings you as much joy as it brought me while finding it.

The Brand

Intentional curation, not mass resale

“Collecting beautiful objects that deserve another life.”

Every piece in the shop is chosen by hand, one hunt at a time. Nothing is bought by the pallet or listed sight-unseen. Each jar is turned over, each plate held up to the light, each candlestick weighed in the palm before it earns a place in the collection. The goal is simple: pieces with history, character, and quality, offered at prices that let them keep living in real homes.
